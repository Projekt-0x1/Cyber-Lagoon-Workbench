# Direct adult runtime causality

**Role:** maintained implementation-facing map for the current Direct runtime.

**Current production semantic baseline:** Target-v5 Revision 12, especially volumes 01–03. This is the vocabulary against which the live Direct tree is explained today, not a claim that its present abstractions are the final or preferred organism design. Foundry/Workbench evidence may justify deleting or replacing those abstractions; when promoted, this maintained baseline must change with the production mechanism.

**Capability authority:** `docs/constitutional_dependency_graph.json`.

**Drift check:** `python3 tools/validate_direct_runtime_causality.py`.

This volume answers one practical question: when a byte crosses the body, where
does authority live, which resident state changes, and which later consequence
may lawfully change it again? It exists so an engineer can trace that causal arc
without reconstructing the whole runtime from thousands of lines of CUDA.

It is not another architecture, a generated code index, or proof that every
described capability is GREEN. Revision 12 defines the intended organism. The
graph and named receipts define what has been earned. Live source defines the
current mechanism. If source and this map disagree, repair the map or the source
in the same causal change; do not preserve a convenient fiction.

## 1. The shortest complete model

```text
Species Gamma
  authored content-free developmental laws and starting Network dispositions
        |
        v
Life Function(Gamma, Xi, Body, Incubator)
  grows one finite resident morphology in a contiguous device arena
        |
        v
DirectBrain
  born matter, resident developmental state, rules, Recipes and resource ecology
        |
        +---- DirectAdultRuntime
        |       execution buffers, frontiers, occurrences, tickets and clocks
        |
raw body contact
  -> resident port resolution
  -> exact contact/participation
  -> propagation and activation
  -> resident action occurrence
  -> motor event
  -> independently returned world consequence
  -> exact settlement/credit/revision opportunity
        |
        v
exact history + checkpoint
  preserve what happened and the complete continuing runtime state
```

Gamma is semantically ignorant, not computationally ignorant. It may author
developmental motifs, affinities, maturation schedules, plasticity and repair
dispositions. It may not contain learned words, concepts, labels, answers,
semantic routes, or expected adult outputs. The Life Function grows the
individual wires; it does not install a mature learned connectome. After birth,
the external compiler is detached and resident development continues as ordinary
mutable tissue.

## 3. State ownership

### 3.1 Born resident matter: `DirectBrain`

The Life Function allocates and compiles one contiguous arena. `DirectBrain`
then exposes views into resident nodes; sparse routes and route incarnations;
delayed-route laws; dense relation blocks; body boundary ports; territory
ancestry; resident fields and rules; Recipe cells, edges and ranges; resident
development and exact history; constructors and construction fronts; persistent
recruited-Network morphology/credit; retention state; counts and roots; and
`DirectResourceEcologyState`.

These bytes are the organism's finite matter. A host may allocate, copy and
checkpoint them, but it may not reinterpret their labels as cognitive meaning.
Every post-birth growth/retraction must pass the resident transaction and finite
resource laws.

### 3.2 Execution state: `DirectAdultRuntime`

The runtime owns the moving execution surface: host/device ingress staging and
rings, ingress credentials, `ResidentActualFrontier`, activation and prediction
buffers, mismatch state, egress and efference, eligibility records and exact
directory, asynchronous tickets, action occurrences and frozen action links,
resident affect/wanting/liking/cue state, the bounded `DirectCausalWorldModel`,
delayed packets, route causal pins, double-buffered participation frontiers and
staging, metrics, configuration, ticks, stream/control state,
persistent-executor authority, archive directory, and observer snapshots.

`DirectCausalWorldModel` is consequence-derived continuing-subject state. Its
relations are updated only from settled verified world returns plus the frozen
`DirectActionParticipationLink`s owned by the emitted action occurrence. An
action may integrate several exact sensory roots. The causal model therefore
resolves the root of each frozen terminal participant independently, deduplicates
the resulting root set, preflights relation/outcome capacity transactionally,
and applies the same verified consequence to every exact root that actually
participated. Each frozen participant must still join its claim and authority
incarnations to resident contact authority at settlement; exact-history replay
cannot substitute for that resident authority. A multi-source action is not
rejected merely because language,
body, memory, or another sensory stream jointly caused it; unrelated or
unresolvable roots still fail closed. The transient participation staging buffer
is not causal-world-model authority and may be cleared at executor phase
boundaries without erasing delayed-consequence ancestry. The model shares the
ordinary consequence-state checkpoint allocation and therefore survives
same-subject capture/restore with affect and wanting/liking state.

Resident Network persistence follows a different boundary from active Network
computation. The hot `ResidentRecruitedNetworkIncidence` row retains sparse
recruitment identity, activation support, consequence credit, bounded counts and
a morphology integrity identity. A separately addressed but equally resident
`ResidentRecruitedNetworkMorphology` row retains the cold reversible constructor:
revision-independent logical Recipe/rank/binding coordinates plus compact
coupling topology. Both banks are finite `ResidentDevelopmentState` matter and
checkpoint together; neither is a sidecar or a serialized latest Network. Exact
Network identity, RecipeRevision, Occurrence membership, participation authority,
bound variables and instantaneous eligibility are reconstructed from current
resident matter.

Multiple distinct Recipe Occurrences may share one exact lived participation
ticket: that shared ticket is causal incidence, while the Occurrence identities
remain the member addresses. After a consequence changes a RecipeRevision, a
later endogenous phase re-observes the live frontier and rebinds the revised
Occurrence instead of copying the old closure forward. Recruitment identity
names revision-independent structural morphology: member logical Recipe
identity, derivation rank and ports, directed formal-variable couplings, and
canonically ordered endpoints for symmetric couplings. Thus consequence may
revise executable matter without destroying a reusable structural recruitment,
while genuinely different topology or source-qualified coupling remains
distinct.

A partial current cue may complete only through consequence-earned recruited
morphology, unfolding missing members as fresh endogenous Occurrences with zero
external authority and zero eligibility. One current Occurrence may participate
in several simultaneous overlapping Networks; a learned completion is therefore
not suppressed merely because its actual cue already belongs to another current
closure. Set accounting counts that shared actual Occurrence once while each
closure retains its own exact Network identity. When several compatible resident
morphologies are available, their existing whole-Network consequence credit is
the only ranking pressure, matching ordinary recruited-Network selection; an
exact best-credit tie remains unresolved. Completion does not mutate the actual
frontier, cannot count as a fresh recruitment observation, and cannot create its
own credit. Stale RecipeRevision, non-executable current morphology or unresolved
competition refuses. Checkpoint may still carry a live execution frontier when
exact mid-computation same-subject continuation requires it; durable learned
authority is the compact Recipe/recruitment constructor, not cached activation or
a semantic/latest-Network object.

### 3.2.1 Research grounding: plastic organism, partial retrieval, and lesion remap

This mechanism is intentionally constrained by organism-level neuroscience rather
than by chatbot/session-memory analogies. The external literature is grounding and
falsifier guidance only; it does not authorize semantic state, named memory
objects, or biology-shaped opcodes.

**Foundry abstraction law: the organism target is durable; implementation ontology
is disposable.** `Recipe`, `RecipeRevision`, `Occurrence`, `Network`, route, node,
"neuron", and every present storage/layout choice are engineering hypotheses, not
constitutional biological entities. Biology constrains causal capacities,
developmental trajectories, resource/robustness trade-offs and falsifiers; it does
not require copying its visible nouns into silicon. Marr-style separation of the
problem, algorithm and implementation warns against confusing a useful current
realization with the computational requirement. Population-dynamics work likewise
shows that behavior may be more naturally explained by collective state evolution
than by assigning a stable semantic variable to each individual neuron. Evidence:
[David Marr, *Vision*](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262288989/vision/),
[Churchland et al. 2012](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature11129), and
[Cunningham & Yu 2014](https://www.nature.com/articles/nn.3776).

The same rule applies below the software level. Neuromorphic and unconventional
computing research explicitly asks what computation can be obtained from native
material dynamics, including analogue, optical, spintronic, memristive and other
non-von-Neumann substrates. Engineering consequence: Direct should preserve a
white-box causal contract while remaining free to replace neuron-like units,
Recipe graphs, explicit Network objects, or digital update machinery with a more
efficient substrate-level dynamical organization if it satisfies the same or
stronger organismal falsifiers. Evidence: [Markovic et al. 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42254-020-0208-2),
[Kumar et al. 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41578-022-00434-z), and
[Jaeger, Noheda & van der Wiel 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-40533-1).

This is also the Foundry interpretation of Sutton and Musk. Sutton's general-method
argument says discovered complexity should come from scalable search/learning,
not researcher-authored domain structure. Musk's engineering sequence requires
questioning the requirement and deleting unnecessary parts before optimization;
optimizing an abstraction that should not exist is a failure. Engineering
consequence: before adding a new resident object or preserving an old one, Foundry
must ask whether the required causal behavior can be expressed with fewer or more
general state variables. Deleting or replacing an implementation abstraction is
preferred when the evidence boundary survives. Evidence: [Sutton 2019, *The
Bitter Lesson*](https://www.incompleteideas.net/IncIdeas/BitterLesson.html) and
[Musk engineering discussion, Starbase 2021](https://elonmuskarchive.org/video/starbase-tour-with-everyday-astronaut-part-1-202).

Therefore architecture review must distinguish two classes explicitly:

- **durable direction:** one continuing embodied organism; ordinary experience;
  causal consequence; source/context lineage; finite resources; plasticity and
  recovery; open-ended language/discourse emerging inside the same organism;
  white-box falsifiability; no privileged semantic/answer oracle;
- **replaceable implementation:** current graph shapes, object names, storage
  layouts, neuron analogies, update equations, solver organization, Recipe/Network
  encodings and hardware realization.

A future implementation is allowed to replace the current machinery wholesale if
it preserves or strengthens the durable direction and re-earns every affected
behavioral/causal proof. Documentation must record the supersession and the new
translation from research constraint to hardware law rather than treating old
implementation vocabulary as sacred.

For the language-mastery arc, that re-earned proof includes a **language phenotype
ratchet**. Every substantive mechanism change must improve at least one measured
emergent Adult language dimension on the same causal path: grounded usability,
composition, coherent span/complexity, ambiguity/noncommit behavior,
source/context calibration, contradiction/relearning, discourse behavior, or
robustness/recovery. Internal cleanup, prettier representations, faster code or a
more biological name is not sufficient by itself. Experience-dependent recovery
is acceptable when biology predicts it, but the before/after comparison may not
weaken no-cheat controls or give the host a semantic answer, target route or hidden
repair instruction. Evidence: [Hillis 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38790398/),
[Tilton-Bolowsky & Hillis 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38514227/), and
[Kong et al. 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39256985/).

- **Sapolsky / experience-dependent plasticity.** Sapolsky's review emphasizes
  that the adult nervous system remains reshaped by experience and that the sign
  and magnitude of plasticity depend on physiological state and history rather
  than a fixed lookup map. Engineering consequence: resident relations may be
  reusable, but current activation and plastic change must remain stateful,
  embodied, and consequence-sensitive. Evidence: [Sapolsky 2003, *Stress and
  Plasticity in the Limbic System*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14584827/);
  see also Stanford's multidisciplinary [Human Behavioral Biology](https://explorecourses.stanford.edu/search?q=BIO+150+Human+Behavioral+Biology) framing.
- **Adult remapping after lost input.** Restricted deafferentation in adult
  primates causes surviving sensory representations to expand into territory that
  lost its former input, demonstrating maintained self-organizing capacity rather
  than restoration of the severed afferent itself. Engineering consequence: a
  lesioned exact route must not be resurrected from memory; fresh authenticated
  traffic may instead establish a new current locator/route through surviving
  resident matter while the old derivation remains historical evidence. Evidence:
  [Merzenich et al. 1983](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6835522/) and
  [Pons et al. 1991](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1843843/).
- **Recovery uses surviving/new circuits.** Post-stroke recovery is better
  characterized as compensation/reorganization through remaining and newly formed
  circuits than as recreation of the exact pre-lesion state. Engineering
  consequence: lesion recovery should be measured as lawful takeover/remapping,
  not byte-identical route restoration. Evidence: [Murphy & Corbett 2009](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn2735)
  and [Nudo & Friel 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10528355/).
  The time course matters: adult-primate retraining after focal infarct preserves
  or expands spared motor representations, and human/animal reviews distinguish
  rapid unmasking from slower use-dependent consolidation and new connectivity.
  Engineering consequence: a focal-lesion assay must not require full intact
  behavior on the first post-lesion tick. It must separately measure immediate
  degradation, survival of durable resident relation morphology, and recovery
  driven by fresh authenticated experience/consequence through surviving matter.
  Evidence: [Nudo et al. 1996](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8650578/),
  [Nudo & Friel 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10528355/), and
  [Hummel & Cohen 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17192704/).
  Because current behavior and plasticity depend on recent state/history, an
  observer probe is itself additional experience. Engineering consequence: an
  *immediate* focal-lesion versus sham comparison across several aliases must use
  independently restored matched pre-probe states; chaining alias probes through
  one control subject would mix the lesion variable with probe-induced state
  change. This isolation rule applies to observer qualification only and must not
  become a runtime reset mechanism. Evidence: the state/history dependence above
  [Sapolsky 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14584827/) and the fact that
  retraining itself changes post-lesion cortical organization
  [Nudo et al. 1996](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8650578/).
  Remapping must also be scored at the right level: preservation of the same
  functional relation does not require invention of a new abstract relation
  identity. Adult cortical studies explicitly show changed physical territory
  supporting recovered function. Engineering consequence: a route-lesion
  falsifier should require the lesioned route/incarnation to disappear from
  current causal participation while the resident relation remains usable through
  different live route matter; it must not demand gratuitous new Recipe IDs as
  evidence of plasticity. Evidence: [Merzenich et al. 1983](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6835522/)
  and [Nudo et al. 1996](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8650578/).
  Nor is recovery required to use the same anatomical/support class: reviews of
  lesion recovery distinguish unmasking of previously inactive pathways,
  redundancy/alternative pathways, axonal sprouting, and new synapses. Engineering
  consequence: the Direct lesion assay must identify the current causal support
  class and ancestry rather than assume a one-for-one sparse-route replacement.
  A lawful resident learned descendant or other resident causal support may carry
  the preserved relation; raw ingress alone may not impersonate remapped learned
  support. Evidence: [Lee & van Donkelaar 1995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8599767/)
  and [Zeiger et al. 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37940595/).
  The assay must distinguish **afferent cause** from **resident implementation**.
  A raw external contact may remain the causal root of a current Occurrence even
  when the central Recipe derivation that realizes that Occurrence has remapped to
  different resident route matter. Engineering consequence: a frozen
  `direct_ingress` action link is not by itself evidence of a shortcut and a
  `sparse_route` action root is not required for recovery. The observer must trace
  the bound Occurrence's current RecipeRevision/route incarnation to a unique live
  resident derivation and prove that implementation is not the lesioned route.
  Evidence: [Merzenich et al. 1983](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6835522/)
  and [Lee & van Donkelaar 1995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8599767/).
- **Degeneracy and spare capacity are robustness mechanisms, not duplicate symbols.**
  Biological degeneracy means structurally different elements can support similar
  outcomes, providing robustness under perturbation; post-stroke aphasia accounts
  likewise identify degeneracy and variable neuro-displacement (recruitable spare
  capacity within/between networks) as candidate recovery principles. Engineering
  consequence: an exact route lesion may invalidate one current executable
  implementation without automatically deleting a consequence-earned higher-level
  recruitment morphology. If another independently current implementation exists,
  it must survive and remain eligible for ordinary competition. If none exists,
  collapse is honest: recovery must come from generic resident construction and
  renewed experience, not from resurrecting the dead route or installing a
  language-specific fallback. A lesion routine that deletes unrelated alternate
  implementations or relation morphology merely because they share one logical
  Recipe conflates implementation damage with knowledge erasure and fails this
  boundary. Evidence: [Stefaniak, Halai & Lambon Ralph 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31772339/),
  [Drion et al. 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34791666/),
  [Stefaniak, Alyahya & Lambon Ralph 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33744459/),
  and [Hillis 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38790398/).
- **Specific injury is not equivalent to low connectivity.** Partial disconnection
  can abolish a function despite substantial surviving connectivity, and recovery
  can reorganize spared pathways. Engineering consequence: an explicitly accounted
  exact-route loss with a nonzero loss identity may nominate bounded generic repair
  even when its source remains above a homeostatic degree floor; spontaneous
  pruning/retraction keeps the ordinary connectivity gate. The injury signal opens
  structural search but does not select a semantic answer or certify a replacement.
  Language recovery must be demonstrated by later ordinary experience and behavior.
  Evidence: [Onifer et al. 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25218468/),
  [Hillis et al. 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24472056/),
  [Cafferty et al. 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26846379/), and
  [Deng et al. 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38607432/).
- **Causal injury state survives bounded plastic-work pressure.** Axonal injury
  responses use timely, information-rich lesion signals to initiate regenerative
  programs; some injury-response signaling remains active until regeneration or
  failure resolves. Engineering consequence: a full bounded construction-work bank
  may not silently drop an authenticated focal-loss identity. Exact injury may
  deterministically retask speculative resident plastic work or a quiescent slot
  before requesting new bookkeeping, while total capacity remains fixed. This
  preemption authorizes repair work only: it does not rewrite learned content,
  choose a semantic answer, or certify the repaired connection as useful. Evidence:
  [Ben-Yaakov & Fainzilber 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19582413/),
  [Hanz & Fainzilber 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16899067/), and
  [Herdegen et al. 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12082227/).
  The current bounded implementation has now earned a language-level phenotype
  delta under that law: the immutable L40S artifact
  `lm2-recipefree-repair-6e3a6ecb02ca` (SHA-256
  `51fc9d3c92bed6b2732ebf5cad53cef933eeb9236629f57974d928dfd13c2c69`)
  moves the accounted focal-lesion LM2 probe from **0/3** usable aliases to **3/3**
  recovered aliases after one quiet endogenous repair epoch. The original physical
  implementation is first lost (`focal_impl_before=1`, `focal_impl_after=0`), a
  fresh resident implementation is rematerialized (`repaired_impl=1`), the taught
  logical relation remains unchanged, and real multi-Occurrence Network actions
  return without probe consequence, expected output, semantic repair instruction,
  or runtime LLM. This is a bounded R0 behavior receipt, not full LM2 capability
  promotion; broader coalition, contradiction/relearning, sparing and sanitizer
  gates remain separate.
- **Language is a network-level organism function.** Reviews of post-stroke
  aphasia describe recovery in terms of spared, degenerate, or previously
  secondary network capacity rather than a single language address. Engineering
  consequence: LM2 lesions must act on generic resident Recipe/Network ecology; a
  language-specific alias table, privileged semantic route, or one canonical
  language locus would contradict the biological target. Evidence:
  [Stefaniak, Halai & Lambon Ralph 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41582-019-0282-1)
  and [Berthier & Pulvermüller 2011](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrneurol.2010.201).
- **Language mastery reuses perception/action/social circuitry.** Neurobiological
  language accounts place learned linguistic forms in distributed circuits that
  reach into perception and action systems, and describe communicative knowledge
  as dynamics within and between those circuits rather than as a detached symbol
  table. Engineering consequence: vocabulary, grammar and discourse must remain
  learned access/coordination over ordinary resident Recipe/Network matter; a
  transcript store, token context, semantic embedding authority, or language-only
  world model would be the wrong causal level. Evidence: [Pulvermüller 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15959465/),
  [Pulvermüller 2017/2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28734837/), and
  [Garagnani & Pulvermüller 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4982106/).
- **Discourse is recurrent joint activity, not monologue plus memory.** Dialogue
  research treats production and comprehension as tightly coupled, with alignment,
  prediction and timing across interlocutors; speaker-listener neural coupling is
  stronger when communication succeeds and includes anticipatory listener activity.
  Engineering consequence: Direct discourse state must be current source-qualified
  relational/predictive organism state that can alter later perception and action.
  It may not be an append-only conversation transcript or a host-selected topic.
  Evidence: [Pickering & Garrod 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15595235/),
  [Pickering & Garrod 2004 refinement](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18241490/),
  and [Stephens, Silbert & Hasson 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20660768/).
- **Every committed motor act may predict its own consequences.** Biological
  forward-model work uses motor efference/corollary signals to predict expected
  sensory consequences and compares those expectations with reafference; speech
  production shows the same architecture in auditory feedback control. Engineering
  consequence: Direct language emission is not exempt from the ordinary motor law.
  A language action may recruit prospective consequence Networks spanning its own
  auditory/body echo, external sensory/social response, and current interoceptive
  state. Those predictions are zero-authority shadows until actual return arrives;
  mismatch may alter only the exact current eligibility/causal chain that produced
  the action. Predicting a following byte may be useful inside surface motor
  realization, but next-byte prediction is not the Adult's objective or semantic
  authority. Evidence: [Shadmehr, Smith & Krakauer 2010](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-neuro-060909-153135),
  [Houde & Nagarajan 2011 / speech forward-model evidence](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4060597/),
  and [Houde & Chang 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25989242/).
- **Punishment and reinforcement are observer descriptions of learned
  contingencies, not resident opcodes.** In operant terminology, punishment means
  a consequence makes the preceding behavior less probable; "positive" means an
  event was added, not that it was beneficial. Engineering consequence: Direct
  must not contain `positive_punishment`, `negative_reinforcement`, embarrassment,
  praise or shame switches. The resident law is action-conditioned prediction,
  independently returned consequence, prediction error, exact causal attribution,
  body/affect participation, and changed future reachability. A later observer may
  classify the resulting contingency as reinforcement or punishment. Evidence:
  [APA: positive punishment](https://dictionary.apa.org/positive-punishment),
  [APA: punishment](https://dictionary.apa.org/punishment), and the neuroscience
  review [Averbeck & O'Doherty 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41386-021-01108-0).
- **Consequence control must reach reusable hierarchical action units.** Biological
  sequence learning does not remain a flat chain of independently reinforced motor
  elements: learned actions are chunked into temporally extended reusable units,
  and reviews explicitly note that simple flat reinforcement accounts do not capture
  goal-directed hierarchical sequence control. Engineering consequence: when a
  consequence settles an utterance produced by a recruited higher-rank language
  Network/span, that recruited unit may carry consequence-earned preference into
  later competition among alternative compositional continuations. Lower surface
  transitions still realize the selected action; they must not be the only level at
  which consequence can alter language. Equal-evidence higher-rank alternatives
  remain ambiguous and fail closed. Evidence: [Garr 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31541637/),
  [Eckstein & Collins 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9274316/), and
  [Jin & Costa 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4523429/).
- **Lived consequence may control sequence persistence as well as choice.** Learned
  action chunks have explicit initiation/termination organization, while basal-
  ganglia/motivational work distinguishes what action is selected from how strongly
  or persistently it is executed. Engineering consequence: Direct language must not
  require a host-authored verbosity or token budget to regulate answer extent. A
  specifically negative consequence attached to an otherwise lawful learned
  continuation may terminate further expansion of that current action sequence;
  unknown or neutral consequence preserves the established sequence law. This is
  action persistence, not semantic censorship, and it may operate only on resident
  consequence-earned matter. Evidence: [Jin & Costa 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4523429/),
  [Dudman & Krakauer 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27012960/), and
  [Salamone et al. 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27189581/).
- **Controllability constrains aversive credit.** Uncontrollable and
  social-evaluative stressors produce stronger and longer stress responses, while
  repeated uncontrollable aversive exposure is associated with learned
  helplessness. Engineering consequence: negative or somatic consequence must not
  become global suppression merely because it followed an action. Credit requires
  the ordinary self-causal/efference and eligibility evidence; unresolved control
  remains uncertainty rather than punishment. Evidence: [Dickerson & Kemeny 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15122924/)
  and [Fernandes, Olsson & Vieira de Castro 2017](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0168159117302095).
- **Agency is learned controllability, not a preinstalled self flag.** Sense-of-agency
  research distinguishes merely producing a movement from learning that one's
  action controls an external event; controllability strongly shapes later
  perception, stress and behavior. Engineering consequence: Direct self-causal
  confidence must grow from repeated joins between an emitted action/efference
  lineage and independently returned consequences whose distribution changes with
  that action. Temporal succession alone is insufficient, and uncontrollable
  outcomes remain uncertainty rather than operant credit. Evidence: [Wen & Imamizu
  2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44159-022-00030-6) and the recent review
  [learned helplessness / controllability 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40933805/).
- **Intrinsic motivation is endogenous regulation and exploration, not a host
  reward channel.** Organisms explore, master and regulate before any externally
  supplied task score; intrinsic-motivation neuroscience links such behavior to
  distributed salience/control/self-referential systems, while allostatic accounts
  describe anticipatory regulation under finite metabolic/computational cost.
  Engineering consequence: Direct may develop action pressure from resident
  uncertainty, novelty, controllability, unfinished causal prediction and finite
  resource state, but the host may not inject a generic curiosity/reward scalar or
  human-like food/hunger fiction. Silicon-specific needs must arise from actual
  resident resource ecology and computation, while stable individual traits must
  be histories of learned dynamics rather than authored personality constants.
  Evidence: [Di Domenico & Ryan 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28392765/),
  [Schulkin & Sterling 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31488322/), and
  [Berridge 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15159167/).
- **Theory of mind is a learned social-prediction layer over ordinary agency.**
  Mentalizing means inferring another agent's beliefs, goals, intentions or other
  latent mental state; current work increasingly models social cognition as
  prediction/inference built from more general perception, action-observation and
  latent-state machinery rather than a transcript parser. Engineering consequence:
  Direct must first learn that other persistent sources have action-conditioned
  consequence distributions, then may recruit Networks that predict those sources'
  future actions or hidden goals. No `belief_of_user`, persona slot, mind-state
  label or language-only social ontology may be authored into Gamma or the host.
  Evidence: [Kliemann & Adolphs 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29529497/),
  [Koster-Hale & Saxe 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24012000/), and
  [Mahmoodi & Rushworth 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41583-026-01028-2).
- **Phenomenology may nominate targets; only primary evidence constrains mechanism.**
  Practical human-behavior sources, including Tony Robbins, may nominate candidate
  phenomena such as state-dependent attention, physiology/focus/language coupling,
  framing, expectancy, identity-consistent action and pattern interruption. Robbins'
  own "Triad" explicitly claims that physiology, focus and language alter state;
  that claim is a hypothesis source, not neuroscience authority. Engineering
  consequence: every such candidate must be checked against primary or high-quality
  review evidence before it becomes Direct law, and unsupported causal stories are
  discarded even when the behavioral observation is useful. Candidate source:
  [Robbins: physiology, focus and language as state levers](https://www.tonyrobbins.com/de/blog/tony-robbins-core-strategies-for-mastering-your-state).
- **Preserve organism function, not evolutionary accidents.** Biological behavior
  contains linked traits, trade-offs and context-dependent maladaptations; evolution
  does not guarantee clean engineering. Engineering consequence: Sapolsky and other
  organism research constrain the functional phenomena and causal interactions we
  need, but no mammalian wiring quirk is sacred. A mechanism may be replaced in the
  workbench when a silicon-native construction preserves or improves the organism
  function, falsifiers and language capability with lower cost or greater
  generality. Evidence: [Sih et al. 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15529965/),
  [Sources of maladaptive behavior 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29274378/),
  and [Trade-offs and constraints 2022](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/717897).
- **The Adult brain is embodiment-agnostic but never body-fictional.** A robot,
  game avatar, browser/computer body or other embodiment owns its actual sensors,
  actuators, resource channels and physical constraints; Direct owns prediction,
  valuation, learning and control over those surfaces. It may not invent mammalian
  food, gut, posture, pain or hormone variables when the attached body exposes none.
  Interoceptive-like influence must arise from whatever internal-resource and body
  state signals are physically available in that embodiment. Evidence for the
  functional principle that internal-state signals bias emotion and action:
  [Mayer 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3845678/),
  [Dunn et al. 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21106893/), and
  [Garfinkel 2025](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-psych-020924-125202).
- **Reference develops through shared attention before mature language.** Joint
  attention coordinates self, partner and a third object/event and supports early
  social learning and vocabulary acquisition. Engineering consequence: later
  discourse reference should emerge from ordinary shared-attention/source/context
  relations and current matter, not from a privileged entity/topic ID authored by
  the host. Evidence: [Mundy & Newell 2007](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2663908/)
  and the critical review [Yu & Smith 2013/2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4258841/).
- **Partial cues can reinstate; updating needs discrepancy/evidence.** Hippocampal
  CA3 literature supports reconstruction from partial cues, while reconsolidation
  work distinguishes retrieval from durable updating and implicates incomplete
  reminders/prediction error in memory modification. Engineering consequence:
  partial contact may transiently complete a consequence-earned resident relation,
  but it must not rewrite durable support or mint credit; durable rebinding remains
  gated by fresh physical participation and independent consequence/mismatch.
  Evidence: [Grande et al. 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3941178/),
  [Sinclair & Barense 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31506189/), and
  [Exton-McGuinness, Lee & Reichelt 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25453746/).
- **Adaptive control consumes finite organism resources.** Sapolsky/McEwen stress
  physiology treats adaptation as state- and history-dependent regulation whose
  short-run protective responses become damaging when activation is excessive,
  prolonged, or fails to terminate; allostasis is stability through change, not
  unlimited capacity. Engineering consequence: bounded Direct queues/frontiers
  must express overload as lawful competition and recovery. Under saturation,
  claimless background traffic may not starve an authenticated current
  sensorimotor lineage that is coupled to the world, and completed episodes must
  be able to release transient load rather than accumulate indefinitely. Evidence:
  [McEwen & Sapolsky 1995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7620309/),
  [Sapolsky et al. 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10696570/), and
  [McEwen 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9629234/).

For LM2, these findings impose a specific non-chatbot rule: **memory may bias or
complete present organism dynamics, but only present embodied traffic and its
consequences may establish which physical route is current after injury.**

Additional engineering discipline comes from two non-biological sources and is
kept strictly subordinate to the organism evidence above:

- **Sutton: general methods discover the contents.** The 2019 *Bitter Lesson*
  argues that durable AI progress comes from general search and learning rather
  than researchers baking their own domain understanding into the agent. The
  2025 Silver--Sutton *Era of Experience* further argues for agents learning in
  long-lived streams from grounded interaction and consequences rather than
  depending primarily on static human-generated data. Engineering consequence:
  Direct may contain generic finite mechanisms for search, competition, credit,
  construction, retention and remapping, but it may not contain an alias table,
  a hand-authored language ontology, a lesion-specific language fallback, or
  researcher-selected answer path. Evidence: [Sutton 2019, *The Bitter Lesson*](https://www.incompleteideas.net/IncIdeas/BitterLesson.html),
  [Sutton publication index](https://incompleteideas.net/publications.html), and
  [Silver & Sutton 2025, *Welcome to the Era of Experience*](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/Era-of-Experience%20/The%20Era%20of%20Experience%20Paper.pdf).
- **Musk: first-principles engineering and negative feedback.** The useful part
  here is methodological, not biological: reduce a novel problem to well-tested
  physical/causal invariants, reason upward from them instead of copying an
  existing product category, and solicit negative evidence early. Engineering
  consequence: each Direct repair must name the invariant it preserves, carry a
  cheap Foundry falsifier before an Adult/GPU proof, and avoid importing a
  conventional chatbot/NLP architecture merely because it is familiar. Evidence:
  [American Physical Society interview, 2013](https://www.aps.org/apsnews/2013/10/profiles-in-versatility)
  and [TED 2013 interview summary](https://blog.ted.com/transforming-transportation-elon-musk-at-ted2013/).

For this project, those constraints explicitly rule out using transformer
attention state, token prediction, next-token loss, corpus pretraining,
backpropagation-through-a-language-model, prompt/session memory, embeddings as
semantic authority, or any equivalent text-native shortcut as the Adult's
language-learning mechanism. Language must remain one embodied experience stream
inside the same persistent organism that learns body, action, consequence and
other relations.

Language remains one learned external-contact and motor-realization ecology, not
the Adult's world model. Lived language Occurrences may participate with and
consequentially reorganize ordinary visual, auditory, motor, affect/body, social
and causal Recipes/Networks. They may thereby later help reinstate or predict
partial cross-modal dynamics, but no declarative fact object, semantic label,
stored image/audio file or language-specific cognition opcode is introduced.

This split is about lifetime, not cognition. Runtime buffers remain part of the
same continuing subject and must be checkpointed when they affect its future.

### 3.3 Host versus resident authority

The host may:

- stage raw body bytes and publish transport cursors;
- choose how many epochs to execute;
- read motor events and transport returned world bytes;
- copy opaque checkpoint/archive bytes and verify their integrity.

The resident/device path alone may:

- resolve a physical body port to resident tissue;
- admit exact contact and causal participation;
- choose resident construction/retraction under its rules and resources;
- bind action occurrences and their exact contributors;
- interpret returned bytes relative to the emitted action;
- attribute causal difference, update resident state, and append exact history.

The host never chooses a cognitive node, route, meaning, correctness label,
reward, participant set or learned answer. Host transport is not cognitive
ownership.

## 4. Birth and continuation

1. `seed_atlas_canonical_species` authors the canonical species-level Gamma

2. `compile_direct_brain` applies Gamma with Xi/body/incubator conditions and

3. `create_direct_adult_runtime` attaches execution state to the born

4. `step_direct_adult_epochs` or `run_persistent_direct_adult_epochs` advances

Stepped and persistent execution may schedule differently, but protected causal
semantics must match: the same admitted inputs, complete starting state and
executor contract yield the claimed replay. Independently lived organisms are
not required to converge to byte-identical later cells, counters or means; they
are judged by an explicitly bounded canalized functional phenotype.

A unique positive actual Network that has not yet earned a motor edge may
originate one bounded exploratory intervention. Its exact Network identity is
mapped across the currently present physical motor ports, so it nominates one
motor rather than every channel; no surface, semantic label, expected output or
host port choice enters that mapping. The emitted action still freezes exact
Occurrence ancestry, and only an independent returned consequence may credit or
revise the recruitment. A newly contacted Network may therefore explore with
zero route eligibility; that zero is frozen unchanged and contributes zero
whole-Network credit rather than being replaced by the exploratory motor
pressure.

Stepped world returns enter through `inject_raw_reafferent_contact` and settle
synchronously through `resolve_consequence_ticket_device` before that call
reports acceptance. They are not a host-authored reward phase hidden inside the
epoch loop. When the emitted action froze one exact recruited Network, settlement
uses the frozen signed Network eligibility as the third factor, records a
`network_credit` exact-history event in the same world-return transaction, and
only after that history commits changes the persistent recruitment credit. Leaf
route/Recipe credit remains separately attributable; neither member activity nor
Network eligibility alone can mint whole-Network credit.
After all consequence-derived mutations commit, the resolver retires only the
exact live actual-participation lineages proven by terminal Occurrences frozen
into that action. Their persistent
RecipeRevisions and recruitment morphology survive, while the completed lived
episode can no longer monopolize later action competition by re-emitting itself.
Before a later sensory batch is inserted, both executors also reclaim sparse
participation rows whose exact frontier lineage is settled and has no live
Occurrence; a live descendant sharing that participation identity protects it.

An action link's route incarnation identifies its current action ancestry; the
Occurrence route incarnation separately identifies the route that constructed
the named Occurrence. They need not be equal when later resident execution has
carried that Occurrence downstream. Settlement accepts this join only under the
explicit authority qualification law: an independent external lineage may
authorize its resident-executed descendant, while a descendant still cannot
manufacture independent external authority. Exact unresolved Network selection
continues to refuse; equal motor-reaching claims are not a selection tie because
all of those physical contributors are frozen into the bounded action closure.

Sparse route transport is one shared cooperative transaction in both executors.
Due packets fold and retire before the current tick proposes new matter. Each
physical route owns one proposal slot and each physical route/participation pair
owns one eligibility-claim slot, so append races cannot discard a lawful
candidate. Arbitration orders physical route chronology from a checkpointed
rotating route cursor, assigns sorted free packet/eligibility slots, and refuses
an indivisible equal-rank cohort when it cannot fit. Physical drive is not
conditional on external-evidence authority: a delayed proposal freezes every
lawful current inherited participation chain, while a nonzero claimless drive
still becomes structural delayed or immediate matter marked
ancestry-incomplete and can mint no eligibility or action authority.
Authority-none current claims are physical sources but never evidence;
zero-delay transport omits them exactly as delayed proposal construction does.
All reserved packet copies complete before
transient proposals are cleared, and final packet plus identity bytes are
revalidated before publication. Malformed packet bytes are released; unlawful
or stale source claims are omitted and cannot become authority. If their
nonzero physical drive remains, its claimless packet stays explicitly
incomplete. Zero-effect attempts do not propagate incompleteness.
At every materializer boundary, the packet frontier is rebuilt as a stable
physical-slot partition: published slots ascend first, free slots ascend next,
and the free head names the first free suffix slot. Restore derives that same
ordering from durable packet states instead of trusting saved allocator order.
Delayed and zero-delay
claims pass through two ordered invocations of the same deterministic batch
admitter; a durable eligibility record is complete before its packed tail is
published to the next participation bank. Exact eligibility identity includes
the ticket, endpoints, physical route and incarnation, context, claim and
authority incarnations, generation-bound parent reference, and ancestry depth.
Ordinary and lineage expiry are mutable lifetime horizons: duplicate producers
merge both monotonically into one record and one generation rather than minting
a second edge. Derived hash/compact-frontier indexes
are canonicalized from durable slots; proposal/claim/scan scratch is rebuilt or
zero at the phase boundary. Neither saved index bytes nor allocator arrival
order are authority. Ticket values and CUDA block scheduling never rank
candidates. The stable canonical #1609 envelope declares aligned
physical bounds P=32768 delayed packets, E=16384 eligibility records, D=4
ancestry edges and 4D=16 frozen action links; its measured peaks exceed every
immediately smaller aligned bound and remain below these bounds without

## 6. One persistent adult epoch

`persistent_direct_adult_kernel` keeps the same causal law inside a cooperative
grid with explicit `grid.sync()` boundaries:

1. snapshot epoch control and advance the resident clock;
2. ingest sensory transport through `device_process_single_sensory_event`,
   commit that exact-history phase, then admit the actual frontier;
3. settle staged returned consequences through
   `resolve_consequence_ticket_device`;
4. execute the same resident development transaction phases, including postbirth condensation followed by the same reserve-first constructor meta-update law used by stepped execution: distributed positive viability across distinct resident ticks consolidates, while sufficiently strong contradiction may reopen without that positive-distribution gate;
5. freeze/carry participation;
6. execute sparse/delayed propagation and dense relations, exhaust the bounded

7. integrate activation;

Persistent execution is not a second cognition path. Shared device primitives
own ingress, action identity and consequence settlement specifically so the two
executors cannot invent different causal laws. A parity receipt must compare the
complete state relevant to the named claim, not merely a matching output count.

## 7. Contact, participation, action and return

### 7.1 Raw contact

`inject_sensory_event` and `inject_actual_sensory_contact` stage transport.
`device_process_single_sensory_event` validates the body-boundary credential and
resolves the declared physical channel/route. Successful admission creates the
resident contact receipt and exact causal participation. Before bounded hot
history can be archived or reused, admission freezes a versioned sensory
authority receipt beside the persistent raw-contact binding: exact ticket and
physical key, committed history sequence/ticks/flags and content root, ingress
chronology, boundary session and authority incarnation. Later ancestry validates
that checkpointed receipt; it neither trusts a raw key alone nor requires the
original hot-page slot to remain resident. Replay and invalid credentials cannot
mint this receipt. A host-supplied valid node index cannot override the body
mapping.

The first authenticated contact may arrive before an exact raw-contact Recipe
incidence exists. In that case it is retained as one bounded pending actual
contact; no Recipe, truth, credit, or semantic relation is fabricated from the
membrane credential. After ordinary sparse/delayed propagation publishes the
current physical `DirectParticipationDescriptor` frontier (`direct_adult_participation_descriptor.cuh`), the resident path may
recruit one structural route Recipe from the exact matching route descriptors.
Competition uses only current physical causal quantities and refuses exact ties.
That structural Recipe has no experiential authority: it merely makes a current
Occurrence available so the same structure can participate in the action whose
later independent consequence may revise it. The exact raw-contact incidence is
then bound and admitted through the ordinary currentness path on a later phase.
This closes the first-experience loop as `contact -> physical propagation ->
structural Occurrence -> action -> consequence -> experience revision`, rather
than creating a Recipe only after the action it was supposed to have caused.

The `ResidentActualFrontier` is bounded. A full live frontier refuses a newcomer
without eviction. Immediate and delayed descendants preserve the source
Occurrence lifetime; delayed arrival may create exact arrival evidence but may
not renew future propagation authority for the same claim.

A transient `ResidentRelationalNetworkClosure` is the connected mathematical
closure over current compatible Occurrences and their shared-variable couplings;
it is not a stored semantic module or membership list. Two Occurrences are
sufficient when they have a valid coupling and boundary. Disconnected pairs
refuse, as do stale identities, incompatible ports, duplicate members, or
uncoupled shared variables. Larger closures use the same law. The closure
identity is canonical under member ordering and may be checkpoint-observed while
current, but durable learned relation state is separate; repeated useful closures
may later earn condensation into RecipeRevisions.

### 7.2 Propagation and activation

Sparse routes, delayed routes and authoritative dense relations are physical
lowerings of resident causal tissue. Backend choice cannot change logical
participation. Approximate or dense state may nominate useful work, but exact
participant identity remains the authority for experiential settlement.

`integrate_adult_node_activation` reads node-local incoming drive, slow context
and resident support. `record_activation_observer` and its basin state observe
the result afterward. The observer has no return path into activation or
learning.

### 7.3 Action occurrence

`bind_action_occurrence` freezes the exact current contribution descriptors into
a bounded action-owned occurrence before motor publication. One Recipe may have
many unresolved Occurrences; settling one cannot overwrite another. If some
ancestry is unrepresented, the action remains explicitly incomplete rather than
fabricating participants. Exact represented contributors remain usable; a
dense-only action with no exact contributor earns no causal credit. An
authenticated direct-ingress descriptor may be the terminal for an
exploratory Network action even though it has no eligibility record. It freezes
the actual Occurrence with zero eligibility; route ancestry and credit are not
invented. When a current Recipe occurrence is exact but broader context remains
unrepresented,
its content may publish only while `MotorEvent.reserved` keeps that incomplete
context explicit. Both stepped and persistent motor origination also fold the
transport phase's target-node incompleteness bit into that warning after the
exact action links have been bound. This warning-only fold cannot erase or
replace an authenticated sibling link: it changes complete to incomplete once,
increments `action_ancestry_incomplete` once, and leaves the frozen participant
offset, count and credit authority untouched. If that action starts a resident
motor trajectory, the checkpointed trajectory freezes the warning separately
from its exact contributors. Every continuation preserves the warning and
counts its own incomplete action once; a later exact rebind cannot upgrade the
physical context of the originating trajectory.

### 7.3.1 Resident public motor trajectory

An exact complete action occurrence may originate one bounded
`ResidentPublicMotorTrajectory`. The resident state freezes only the physical
motor port, extent/cursor and exact current contributor identities. It contains
no output Word, byte sequence, string, transcript or expected result. Cursor
zero is expressed by the originating action exactly once; each later ordinary
tick revalidates every contributor and re-executes the selected current
Occurrence through its current RecipeRevision morphology before advancing the
cursor. One fixed runtime-owned, resource-charged device slot and the configured contributor capacity
provide finite ownership: a live trajectory cannot be overwritten, and an
over-capacity origin refuses.

Stepped and persistent executors call the same continuation primitive. A live
continuation owns the motor-publication phase even under temporary ticket or
efference backpressure, so host call cadence cannot inject competing origin
work. Each continuation reconstructs its terminal eligibility descriptors from
their exact current slot/generation and freezes the complete verified sensory
root-to-motor closures into that new action occurrence; the resident trajectory
still orders only the terminal Recipe Occurrences and never substitutes a
terminal-only credit path. Its frozen origin warning is ORed into every
continuation without changing those exact closures. Missing, stale, rebound,
incomplete or physically displaced contributors fail closed. Each emitted
`MotorEvent` carries an immutable lineage artifact;
that observer record has no resident input API and cannot affect later
selection, morphology, scheduling or evidence. Motor history, own output and
reafference likewise cannot become new trajectory contributors.

### 7.4 Returned world consequence

`resolve_consequence_ticket_device` accepts only the exact pending ticket/action
identity and valid incarnations. It compares the returned raw word with the
emitted word, appends the exact world-return history before authoritative
mutation, settles that occurrence once, and applies causal difference through
its frozen links. Credit is causal attribution—not reward, liking, truth or an
answer key—and remains separate from the later choice of update.
Settlement then retires the action's exact frozen actual-participation
lineages. Alternatives in that completed lineage receive no credit unless they
have their own frozen action link; unrelated live lineages remain current, and
proposals or predictions cannot name a lineage for retirement.

Before that history commit, a bounded pure preflight stages every affected
RecipeCell revision, same-cell derivation and incidence, and every live
Occurrence/work/output rebind. Route-gain work is evaluated against the exact
recorded conductance delta; condensed representations refuse because their
proof revisions cannot be rewritten without a new witness. Only after the
complete history phase is durable are the route, Recipe, derivation, incidence,
Occurrence and work snapshots published by infallible assignment. Thus an
accepted ticket-bound return cannot leave a current RecipeRevision paired with
a stale live Occurrence, including when the returned word exactly matches the
organism's emitted word.

Unknown, stale, expired and duplicate returns fail closed. Reafference from the
organism's own output is evidence of that causal loop, not independent external
corroboration.

## 8. Development, resources and identity

Resident development is ordinary adult tissue after birth. It may mature,
propose, filter, scan, reserve, commit and repair under Gamma-authored laws, but
no external Life Function remains as a semantic controller. Route incarnation
is part of causal identity; slot reuse without incarnation change is ABA aliasing
and is forbidden.

## 9. Exact history, cold transport and checkpoint

`DirectExactHistoryHotPage` is arena-owned resident state. Exact event records
are appended in resident causal order and cannot wrap over old history. Cold
archive code in `archive_direct_exact_history_page` moves resident-addressed
immutable bytes durably; the host may not select, reorder, reinterpret or replay
them as fresh witness. A fixed-morphology execution surface with no resident
development owner has no hot page to archive, so paging is a no-op rather than
fabricating history state.

`DirectAdultCheckpoint` captures the arena plus every runtime buffer/frontier,
configuration, transport cursor and tick that can affect the future. Capture and
restore verify the payload digest before accepting state. Checkpoint identity is
therefore a same-subject continuation law for the same complete state, executor
and ordered future inputs. It is not proof that two separately lived adults must
be microscopically identical.

The resident motor-trajectory slot is one of those ordinary checkpoint buffers.
Its cursor and frozen contributor identities preserve future causal selection;
checkpoint bytes never cache or replay future motor output.

Archive/history answers “what was recorded?” Checkpoint answers “what complete
state continues?” Neither alone proves resident recall, interpretation,
contradiction revision, language mastery or adult capability.

## 10. Canonical developmental language-contact bridge

exact history while preserving the bounded functional law that each opaque
contact remains separately selectable; separately lived states are not required
to become byte-identical.

Each physical input node retains one semantics-blank bootstrap incidence for
an unseen raw contact. Later derivations cannot replace or outrank that

and route incarnation. Distinct exact raw-contact keys remain additive, while
one exact key has only one current incidence locator. A malformed state with
multiple bootstrap incidences for one node or multiple exact locators for one
raw-contact key is ambiguous and fails closed rather than selecting by array or
derivation order.

An exact incidence is recorded only when its authenticated raw-contact node,
incidence trigger node, sparse causal source, and executable input port are the
same physical node. A mismatched sparse participant is skipped before it can
allocate a Recipe derivation or change a route builder; later lawful
participants remain eligible. Direct and checkpointed pending admission reject
legacy exact incidences that violate this node identity instead of borrowing an
internal activation from another source. If the unique exact locator is instead
well-formed but noncurrent because its route/revision has been physically
lesioned, retracted or superseded, fresh authenticated contact may use current
resident propagation to recruit one unique executable replacement derivation.
Only then may the current exact-incidence locator move to that replacement; the
prior derivation and exact history remain resident, no second exact key is
created, and ambiguous or wrong-node replacement refuses atomically.

Sparse eligibility records freeze a generation-bound parent reference, the
nonrenewing lineage horizon of the root claim, and bounded ancestry depth. A lawful
refresh of that same authoritative root may extend the existing record horizon;
transport hops cannot add lifetime of their own. Descendants inherit the
current parent horizon across immediate and delayed transport, while an
already-frozen shorter child remains bounded by its original horizon. Arrival
may still carry physical drive after expiry, but it cannot renew external causal
credit.
At a resident motor action the executor walks only those exact parent
references, validates current route endpoints/incarnations and the verified
sensory-history/raw-binding root, and freezes the complete root-to-motor closure
into a separately bounded action-link owner. Each link retains the contributor's

four physical terminal slots

### 10.1 Mixed-rank affine range

Recursive language Merge uses the same mixed-rank whitebox fold that mints
`p(n+1)`. A minted Recipe stores the flattened affine in `int32` Q16.
`direct_whitebox_exact_q16_mul` is already exact: it multiplies in `int64`,
requires a Q16 multiple, and refuses a result outside `int32`. That refuse is
law, not a rounding bug.

A shallow compose of two minted Recipes can still fold. Composing the flattened
handle of that mint with another affine, or folding the three-member source
path `src ∘ p(n+1) ∘ p(n+2)`, can overflow in the **result**. Saturating or
widening storage would change Adult exactness. `c.whitebox_exact_condensation`
does not claim unbounded recursive depth. Unbounded phrase∘phrase therefore
needs a later planted exact-range pack, not a workbench token and not a
translation table.
